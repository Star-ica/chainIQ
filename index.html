HOW IT WORKS
Three steps from idea to paid winners
01
Creator deposits reward pool
Configure your quest — title, task description, reward per winner, max winners, and deadline. Send BOT to lock the pool on-chain.
02
Participants submit proof
Anyone with a wallet can submit an IPFS CID, transaction hash, tweet URL, or any proof link. Each submission is timestamped on-chain.
03
Creator approves, reward transfers
Review each submission and approve winners. BOT transfers atomically to the winner's wallet — no intermediate steps, no manual transfers.
FEATURES
Built different from Web2 quest tools
🔒
Non-custodial reward pools
Funds live in the smart contract, not a platform multisig. Creators can cancel and reclaim at any time.
⛓
Immutable proof record
Every submission is recorded on BOT Chain with a timestamp. No one can alter or delete the history.
⚡
Atomic payouts
Approving a submission and transferring the reward happen in a single transaction. No settlement lag.
🔓
Permissionless creation
Any wallet can create a quest. No sign-up, no approval process, no account required.
↩
Post-deadline refunds
If your quest ends with unfilled winner slots, reclaim the remaining pool in one transaction.
📊
Transparent fee
A flat 2% platform fee is calculated and shown before you sign. Nothing hidden, nothing variable.
